# Bypass N4L

To bypass N4L's restrictions, you'll need to download a piece of software called a VPN (Virtual Private Network), which connects you to a server located around the world and all your internet traffic goes through it instead of directly through your current connection. This page lists the VPNs that currently allow connections on networks supplied by N4L.

Mutliple mirrors are supplied for each download as N4L regularly adds sites hosting VPN downloads to their blacklists.

## Tor

Nuclear option. Won't connect unless you use a `meek-azure` or `webtunnel` bridge, which can be obtained from here.
