# Palo Alto Changeover

Network for Learning is changing their spyware solution from [Fortinet](../fortinet.html) to Palo Alto, meaning that students' data will be fed into an AI learning model.

## Why

It is believed that N4L wants tighter control over student internet access[1](https://www.reseller.co.nz/article/3497119/n4l-targets-cyber-security-and-standardised-software-deployments-in-schools.html) (more "centralised"). This will allow them to collect more student information and data, allowing them to train their AI models to recognise VPN traffic so it can be blocked.

## Timeline

At the start of 2026, N4L started offering a new spyware certificate for use with Palo Alto.
