# Fortinet

|:-----------------|:-----------------------|
| **Founded** | 2000 |
| **Purpose** | Invade student privacy |
| **Supported by** | [N4L](./n4l/index.html) |

Fortinet is a piece of software designed to restrict access to web pages, commonly used in tandem with [Network for Learning](./n4l/index.html).

## Legality

Censorship is something only a government can do, and while there is valid reasons why content should be blocked (such as NSFW), Fortinet takes this to another level similar to that of Kwangmyong or the Great Firewall of China. This allows full control over internet-based activities.

## What to do

*See [N4L's section on "What to do](./n4l/index.html)"*

N4L is ditching Fortinet for Palo Alto when they swap ISPs from Spark to 2degrees later this year, which is considerably worse than Fortinet due to it's abhorrent AI, with the best comparison being the Great Firewall of China.
