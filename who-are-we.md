# Who are we?

We're Freedom for Students and we fight for students rights in restrictive school networks. Being based in New Zealand, we know that internet freedom is something we take for granted and something that schools should provide free access to, but some take this "free" to another level. When our anonymous founder discovered what N4L was doing, they started a movement known as "Call of Freedom", now referred to commonly as "Freedom for Students" (shortened to "F4S" to annoy N4L).

We're aware that some of our claims, such as the comparison of N4L to Kwangmyong and the Great Firewall of China, are a bit hyperbolised.
