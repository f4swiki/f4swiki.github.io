# ProtonVPN

ProtonVPN is our favourite way of bypassing [N4L's](../spyware/n4l/index.html) totalitarian regime behind networks using the Palo Alto firewall. Between 19th August 2025 and 12th June 2026, we did not recommend this VPN due to it's partial blockage, which has since been resolved.

## Using ProtonVPN

Make sure to use the "Smart" (enable "Proton Protocols") or "WireGuard UDP" protocol.

## Blocking attempts

In late 2024, N4L successfully disabled ProtonVPN's ability to receive server lists, effectively disabling the VPN. This was bypassed roughly 24 hours later.

On July 21 2025, N4L's blocking software was updated to a newer version, making it able to block connections on all of ProtonVPN's protocols. We (F4S/CoF) sent a polite email on 22 July 2025 to ProtonVPN, who likely added new servers on new IPs. This solved the problem for roughly two days. Many connections to Proton's IPs were blocked, with only [a few servers](../archive/working-protonvpn-servers.html) able to be connected to.

ProtonVPN was fully blocked for a period between February 2026 and May 2026, although Proton has found a workaround.
